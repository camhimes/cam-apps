Glance Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Glance is a personal health dashboard for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what it does with your data.

What Glance reads

With your permission, Glance reads these items from Apple Health:

  • Sleep analysis
  • Workouts (strength training, running and cycling)
  • Resting heart rate

How it's used

Glance uses this data only to show your sleep averages, weekly lift and cardio progress, and resting heart rate trends in the app and in watch widgets.

Where it stays

All data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Glance shares data between your own devices through Apple's Watch Connectivity and a shared app container. It has no accounts, servers or analytics. It doesn't send your data to the developer or to anyone else.

What Glance doesn't do

  • It doesn't collect, sell or share your data.
  • It doesn't use your data for advertising or marketing.
  • It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • It doesn't write to Apple Health.

Your choices

You can change or revoke Glance's access at any time in the Health app (Sharing › Apps › Glance) or in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices. Deleting the app removes the data it stored.
